# Hong Kong Lending Controls and Suspicion Reporting

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update adds licensed-lender controls, August 2026 changes and observed supervision findings. It separates loan decisions, licence compliance and suspicion reporting rather than treating them as interchangeable outcomes.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** A money-lender licence alone does not make its holder an AMLO-defined financial institution. Licence conditions nevertheless require AML controls. [Statute][S01], [conditions][S02].
- **Fact:** First-phase unsecured-lending reforms took effect on 1 August 2026; the credit-information phase is scheduled for June 2027. [Government][S04].
- **Fact:** Official rectification/warning totals cover licensing outputs, not solely AML findings. [CR orders][S06], [CR warnings][S07].
- **Analysis:** Lender training should distinguish an affordability exception from grounds for suspicion while preserving evidence that can connect the two.

## Latest Regulatory Developments

| Requirement / publication | Impact | Who is affected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current AMLO Schedule 1 definition | Has no standalone money-lender category. Another regulated activity may separately bring an entity within the definition. [S01][S01]. | Firms identifying their regulatory perimeter. |
| April 2026 licensing guide, effective 1 August | Condition 14 incorporates the Registrar's AML guideline by reference to AMLO Schedule 2. Conditions 15–16 address recorded affordability and low-income borrower controls; numbering can vary on a licence. [S02][S02]. | Licensed lenders. |
| First-phase reforms, 1 August | Ban requested loan referees and cap total monthly unsecured repayments/income at 35% up to HK$6,000 income and 40% at HK$6,001–12,000. Credit Data Smart submission phase is scheduled for 1 June 2027. [S04][S04]. | Lenders and affected unsecured borrowers. |
| March 2025 lender AML guideline | Prompt MLRO routing, documented evaluation, reporting on formed knowledge/suspicion including attempts, and review of the ongoing relationship after reporting. [S03][S03]. | Frontline staff, MLROs and management. |

**Fact:** The guideline rejects unrelated managers filtering internal reports, requires evaluation/report records, and explains that consent is not a clean bill of health. [S03][S03].

**Analysis:** An app does not create its own regulatory category. A credit decision, complaint, licence breach, fraud allegation and STR each require the relevant facts and route. A lender should not infer the statutory perimeter solely from the product's user interface.

## Market Trends

**Facts:** Audit Report 85, published 5 November 2025, found delayed annual risk profiling and use of old profiles. A new lender could potentially finish a 12-month licence without assigned risk/routine onsite monitoring. Inspections found missing third-party-agreement responses, loan-explanation evidence and affordability records. Of 191 follow-ups in 2024–25, 131 came after two months; CR described that period as a general expectation and accepted recommendations. These are dated findings, not proof that they remain unresolved. [S05][S05].

| Consolidated CR / Police Licensing Office outputs | 2025 | January–August 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rectification orders | 377 | 432 |
| Warning letters | 22 | 29 |

The tables leave later 2026 months blank. They do not report zero for those months, count unique non-compliant firms or identify every output as AML-related. [S06][S06], [S07][S07].

**Fact:** March 2026 consultation conclusions separately report 51 thematic inspections in 2025, 27 breaches, 23 orders and one warning. An advertisement-review subset covered 199 advertisements, 24 enquiries and 20 orders by 10 March. Do not add these subsets to the consolidated totals. [S08][S08].

**Historical case:** ICAC reported a Universal (Asia) Credit manager's 19-month sentence on 31 May 2024 for fraud/attempted fraud and laundering conspiracy involving false referral rebates. Conduct was in 2015–2020: over HK$270,000 paid and about HK$50,000 attempted but unprocessed. A corruption complaint initiated the investigation; no STR or monitoring alert is identified. This is not a 2026 conviction. [S09][S09].

## AML Technology Landscape

**Fact:** The lender guideline addresses impersonation safeguards for non-present customers, with its recognised reliable/independent digital-identification exception. A technology check needs to address the relevant risk. [S03][S03].

**Fact:** The November 2025 JFIU lender webinar illustrates agent-linked forged applications, unrelated repayment parties/unknown funding and early lump-sum repayment beyond supported income. These are educational typologies, not measured product detections or labelled conviction outcomes. [S10][S10].

| Proposed review task | Potential evidence | Distinct decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test the stated repayment capacity | Income, debt commitments, employment term and recorded assessment. | Loan approval / licence compliance. |
| Enquire about an unexpected repayment | Payer identity, relationship, funding explanation and relevant records. | Whether the explanation resolves suspicion. |
| Compare agent-linked applications | Supported connections and document inconsistencies. | Internal escalation and MLRO evaluation. |
| Check remediation evidence | Required action, completion record and reviewer rationale. | Whether a control defect has been addressed. |

These are analysis of useful tasks. Affordability thresholds do not automatically determine whether an STR is required, and a shared agent alone does not establish a criminal scheme.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** Documentation and escalation exercises may help lenders whose teams must apply credit, licensing and reporting controls together. Potential users include lending operations, compliance reviewers and educators. Published enforcement outputs do not establish willingness to buy, software failure rates or returns on investment.

**Recommended next steps:** Interview teams about handoffs between credit review and MLRO evaluation; inspect synthetic examples of the missing evidence they repeatedly request. Compare existing training and remediation tracking before proposing an additional product. A pilot should assess supported reasoning, appropriate enquiries and routing, rather than score all early repayments as laundering.

**TwinAML proposal:** A synthetic agent-linked loan case could offer credible and contradictory repayment explanations, with separate loan and reporting choices. Reuse the current evidence/timeline model if implementation is later authorised. No real borrower records, private STRs or lending systems were accessed.
