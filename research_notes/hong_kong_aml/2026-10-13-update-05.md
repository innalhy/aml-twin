# Hong Kong Family Offices and Wealth Evidence

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update expands the [baseline's wealth-management coverage](2026-10-13-baseline.md#market-trends). It separates licensing, AML duties, tax eligibility, and proportionate corroboration. The current tax-enhancement Bill's enactment was not established.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** A family-office label does not settle SFC licensing. Regulated activity, a business, and Hong Kong operations must all be assessed. [SFC FAQ][S01].
- **Fact:** Tax eligibility and licensing use different ownership tests. The ordinary tax test's 95% family interest does not establish the SFC Type 9 wholly owned intra-group carve-out. [IRD][S04], [SFC][S01].
- **Fact:** HKMA expects proportionate wealth/funds corroboration and warns against excessive or repeated historical document demands. [2023 Smart Tips][S07], [2024 circular][S08].
- **Analysis:** Useful tooling should make evidence and uncertainty reviewable while reducing unnecessary customer requests. More collected documents alone would not prove better compliance.

**Facts:** The currently accessible IRD explanation describes the 2023 FIHV regime, applicable from 2022/23: a HK$240 million specified-asset threshold, relevant Hong Kong management/core activities, at least two qualified full-time employees and HK$2 million local expenditure. Ordinarily the family holds at least 95% in both the FIHV and eligible office, subject to charitable-entity provisions; at least 75% of the office's assessable profits must come from specified family services. Beneficial-owner records must be ascertainable. [IRD eligibility and records][S04]. These are tax conditions; they do not provide an SFC licensing threshold.

**Analysis:** Keep separate records for licensing scope, service-provider AML obligations, suspicion reporting, and tax eligibility. A tax ruling should not automatically clear investment-service licensing or a bank's CDD. The [baseline JFIU reporting discussion](2026-10-13-baseline.md#latest-regulatory-developments) remains relevant to persons outside a licensing perimeter; absence of an SFC licence requirement is not a general removal of suspicion-reporting duties.

**Verification boundary:** The official LegCo search-indexed Bill entry contains early-stage dates but no later passage fields in the retrieved evidence. Direct full-page retrieval failed. This is limited evidence, not proof that no subsequent legislative event occurred. [Bill record][S09].

**Later verification:** [Update 11](2026-10-13-update-11.md) records successful live-page retrieval and the official 30 July committee minutes. This resolves the access limitation above while leaving enactment and commencement unverified.

## Market Trends

**Fact:** In July 2025 government distinguished the specific single-family FIHV concession from the unified fund regime, under which qualifying vehicles served by licensed multi-family offices may obtain exemption. It reported few early applications and withheld detailed taxpayer data. The estimated 2,700 offices were not 2,700 concession users. [Government reply][S05].

**Analysis:** A count of offices is neither a count of regulated buyers nor an AML software budget. Segment prospective customers by activities, bank relationships, structures, staff and review volume before estimating demand.

**Facts:** HKMA's 2023 tips distinguish the origin of total wealth from the particular funds entering a relationship. Collection, clarification and corroboration should match assessed risk; public information and reasonable estimates can help. Its examples include auditable AI with attention to data quality, lineage and local context. [Smart Tips][S07]. The 2024 circular warns against excess or duplicate requests and demands for records dating back years, and against presenting disproportionate requests as legal requirements. [Circular][S08].

**Analysis:** A defensible workflow must handle both directions of error: accepting an unsupported material claim and burdening a legitimate customer with unnecessary requests. Read this alongside the [baseline's high-end laundering findings](2026-10-13-baseline.md#latest-regulatory-developments); proportionality does not justify closing unresolved concern.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** A narrow review aid for private-bank/wealth teams and service providers could map each material assertion to its owner/contributor, date, estimated amount, evidence, assumptions, contradiction and review decision. It should recommend focused follow-up from gaps and changes; it should not manufacture evidence or automatically treat every missing historic document as suspicious.

**Recommended next steps:** Interview reviewers about repeated requests, material-change reviews, difficult structures and the time spent explaining decisions. Compare the proposed workflow with current bank systems and corporate KYC tools. In a pilot measure unsupported claims accepted, legitimate explanations rejected, duplicate requests, review time and reasoning completeness. Validate licensed/public data access and privacy requirements before using real customer information.

**TwinAML training proposals, not legal precedents:**

1. An office meets a tax ownership percentage but seeks to rely on a wholly owned group licensing carve-out. The learner must request the structure and business facts; the tax percentage alone cannot decide the answer.
2. A founder's wealth has credible public support. The learner chooses material corroboration and avoids repeatedly requesting decades of statements solely to complete a checklist.
3. A new contributor funds a family vehicle through a poorly explained route. The learner separates account-routing evidence from the explanation of wealth generation, records uncertainty and chooses a reasoned follow-up or escalation.

These can be specified as synthetic evidence exercises within TwinAML's existing rules-before-AI model. No case, application, licence determination or live customer review was implemented in this update.
