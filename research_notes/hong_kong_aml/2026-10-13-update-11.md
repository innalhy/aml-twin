# Hong Kong Legislative Status and Deposit Authorisation Controls

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update adds newer dated evidence to [update 5](2026-10-13-update-05.md) and [update 7](2026-10-13-update-07.md), resolves an earlier Bill-page retrieval limitation, and adds the August SFC simplified-eDDA circular. Legislative status remains bounded by the records actually obtained.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** A 16 September government statement still describes four new VA service regimes as legislative proposals, targeting a Bill within 2026. This is newer than the May/June evidence; enactment was not verified. [Government][S01].
- **Fact:** Live retrieval of the preferential-tax Bill record succeeded. The committee's 30 July minutes establish completed clause examination, while later legislative steps remain unresolved in the retrieved records. [LegCo record][S02], [minutes][S03].
- **Fact:** The SFC's 20 August circular requires firms to assess simplified-eDDA authorisation and financial exposure, alongside suspicious deposit behaviour. [SFC][S06].
- **Analysis:** Training should distinguish identity, account ownership, consent, transaction context, legal status and reporting decisions. A successful payment setup does not answer all six questions.

## Market Trends

**Facts:** The September government account also describes August launch of the first regulated stablecoin. This corroborates the timing of [update 7's issuer beta evidence](2026-10-13-update-07.md#market-trends), without establishing general retail availability or volume. [S01][S01].

**Analysis:** Legal frameworks, issuer rollout and intermediary payment controls are progressing on different timelines. Buyers may need to track these together, while avoiding a single status label that hides the actual legal entity, activity and operational stage.

**Facts:** SFC flags new eDDA/new-wallet combinations and unusual deposits rapidly converted into VA and withdrawn. Appropriate responses include withholding or declining instructions, investigating and assessing reporting. Supplementary limits/authentication or alternative deposit methods may be needed. [S06][S06].

**Analysis:** These are investigation triggers, not proof of laundering or an automatic STR rule. Deposit acceptance, fraud response, notification to a supervisor and suspicion reporting must retain their own evidence and decision records. The [broker reporting distinctions in update 10](2026-10-13-update-10.md) remain relevant.

## AML Technology Landscape

| Existing mechanism | Strength | Weakness or evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank-mediated authorisation and ownership evidence | Can provide evidence from the institution processing the underlying account. | Successfully sending instructions is different from establishing informed authorisation. Evidence still needs reconciliation with the customer profile. |
| Deposit, account-change and wallet-event monitoring | Can expose combinations whose significance is missed when each event is reviewed alone. | A sequence alone does not determine intent; legitimate explanations and missing facts must remain visible. |
| Legislative version tracking | Can separate consultation, Bill, passage, commencement and transition. | Current-index completeness must be verified; an empty field or an inaccessible page is weak absence evidence. |
| Evidence-linked case review | Could preserve where ownership, consent and transaction-context conclusions came from. | Product hypothesis; integration effort and customer demand are not established by a circular. |

The table is an analytical comparison, not a named-vendor procurement finding or a claim that a new product satisfies the circular.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** Brokers and VATPs could use a training exercise that combines payment setup, identity records, direct bank evidence, new wallet registration and subsequent transfer instructions. The learner must identify which evidence supports each conclusion, record gaps, select proportionate follow-up and explain separate control/reporting decisions.

**Recommended next steps:** Interview operations and compliance reviewers about hand-offs and disputed authorisation, compare existing account-registration and payment systems, then evaluate a synthetic exercise. Measure overlooked contradictions, unnecessary restrictions, quality of rationale and correct use of authoritative evidence. No live payment control or real-customer decision was implemented here.

**Continuing status checks:** Obtain a matching enacted ordinance and commencement instrument for the four VA regimes and preferential-tax enhancement before changing their status. Obtain the actual third HRA and its assessed period before adopting new sector ratings. These checks remain open rather than being inferred from announcement wording.
