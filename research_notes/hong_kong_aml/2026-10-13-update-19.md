# Hong Kong Data Foundations for AML Monitoring

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update adds the HKMA's 12 October publication to the adoption findings in [update 17](2026-10-13-update-17.md). It examines data assurance behind monitoring; it does not establish a new AML software licence, compulsory product or measured detection improvement.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** The 12 October circular asks Authorized Institutions (AIs) to take account of industry good practice in data governance, quality and infrastructure. [Circular][S01].
- **Fact:** Its enclosed guide complements supervisory requirements without prescribing a separate requirement set or one implementation model. [Guide][S02].
- **Fact:** An illustrative KYC lineage example combines authoritative metadata with checks on a language model's output. [Guide][S02].
- **Analysis:** The opportunity is to make data defects and their consequences reviewable. Better explanations alone cannot establish that the underlying customer or transaction records are reliable.

**Boundary:** “Good practice” should retain that status when translated into a procurement brief or training exercise. The publication is not evidence that every bank must implement the example architecture, or that the HKMA has approved a named supplier for its AML system.

## Market Trends

**Fact:** The circular discusses automated quality controls and metadata capabilities alongside organisational accountability. [S01][S01].

**Analysis:** This expands the earlier adoption discussion from model selection to the inputs on which a monitoring decision depends. An alert-review team may receive a polished summary while an incomplete transaction feed or stale ownership record remains unresolved. Those defects require an identified owner and a correction route; they cannot be resolved by a more confident narrative.

The publication documents supervisory attention. It supplies neither the number of banks buying these tools nor an AML-specific budget. The barriers and adoption cohorts in update 17 therefore remain the appropriate dated evidence for those separate questions.

## AML Technology Landscape

**Facts:** Figure 6 shows plain-language access to a lineage repository, retrieval from authoritative metadata and accuracy checks before reliance. Its example traces a field to a KYC document. Section 4.2 also addresses controls at capture, interfaces and outputs, abnormal volume/distribution, exception handling and remediation responsibility. Named sources in the illustration are not a disclosed bank contract. [S02][S02].

| Proposed investigation exercise | What to assess | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing transactions from a simulated feed | Whether the reviewer notices the coverage gap and requests the relevant records. | A quiet alert queue does not show low underlying risk. |
| Different ownership records in two systems | Whether the reviewer establishes dates, sources and the supported version. | A merged record can preserve an incorrect association. |
| A generated explanation cites the wrong document | Whether the reviewer checks the citation and corrects the conclusion. | Plausible prose does not prove provenance. |
| A correction fails to reach downstream monitoring | Whether the reviewer identifies affected decisions and the responsible team. | Correcting one screen does not establish propagation. |

These are proposed synthetic scenarios, not incidents reported in the guide. They extend the evidence-review themes of [update 14](2026-10-13-update-14.md) and the correction exercise in [update 15](2026-10-13-update-15.md).

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** Compliance/data teams may value a focused assurance exercise that connects a data defect to an investigation decision. The buyer and budget could sit across operations, risk and technology rather than solely with an MLRO. That allocation is a customer-research question.

**Recommended next steps:** Ask reviewers which fields cause repeat enquiries, who can correct them and how they confirm the change reached monitoring. Compare the proposed exercise with existing data-quality dashboards and audit work. A small synthetic pilot could measure justified source selection, detection of missing evidence and correct escalation; it should not claim real laundering detection or institution-wide compliance effectiveness.

**TwinAML scope:** Reuse the existing case timeline and evidence choices if this is later authorised for implementation. A synthetic conflicting document or incomplete feed can teach the point without building an enterprise catalogue or connecting real bank data. No application changes were made for this update.
