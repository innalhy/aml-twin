# FATF Standards, Hong Kong Assessment and Rule Versions

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update refines the [baseline international comparison](2026-10-13-baseline.md#latest-regulatory-developments). Adoption of a standard, assessment against it and commencement of a Hong Kong obligation are separate events.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** FATF's current Hong Kong page still separates 2023 technical ratings from 2019 effectiveness findings. A follow-up rerating is not a new effectiveness evaluation. [Country page][S01], [2023 release][S02].
- **Fact:** FATF's calendar gives possible November 2029 onsite and June 2030 plenary months for Hong Kong. These are indicative, not fixed appointments. [Calendar][S03].
- **Fact:** Revised R16 has an end-2030 readiness horizon. The adopted payment assessment annex is marked not yet effective, with its assessment effective date to be decided. [Consultation][S06], [methodology][S04].
- **Fact:** Hong Kong's trust-customer CDD amendment and company-held SCR are concrete local measures, but do not alone establish complete implementation of revised R24/R25. [SFC][S12], [CR][S13].
- **Analysis:** Rule-version and evidence-provenance controls are practical training requirements. An international change must not silently become a current local decision rule.

### Payment fields: current bank rule versus future international assessment

**Current Hong Kong facts:** HKMA's repository labels May 2023 AML-2 as current. [Record][S07]. For bank wires of at least HK$8,000, paragraph 10.5 requires party names/account or reference identifiers plus the originator's address **or** customer/document ID **or**, for an individual, date and place of birth. Below that threshold party names/identifiers remain required. Domestic transfers have a traceable-reference/data-availability arrangement. [Chapter 10][S08].

**Adopted future assessment facts:** Annex IV criterion 16.6 adds originator address, beneficiary country/town, natural-originator birth date and available legal-entity identifiers above the applicable threshold. Criterion 16.21 permits at least one of transaction-level alignment, holistic risk-based monitoring or shared pre-validation; name alignment need not be exact. [Methodology Annex IV][S04].

**Analysis:** These are different versions and scopes. Do not convert a planned international standard into a live Hong Kong threshold/field requirement, or claim it mandates a particular recipient-verification product. “Required information complete” and “suspicion resolved” also require separate judgments. The existing [VA transfer rules in update 7](2026-10-13-update-07.md#latest-regulatory-developments) need their own role/scope record.

### Beneficial ownership: local measures versus jurisdiction-level standards

**Facts:** SFC's 24 May 2023 circular explains AMLO changes enacted in December 2022 and effective 1 June 2023. Trust-customer beneficial-owner definitions remove the old 25% threshold and add trustees, beneficiaries and beneficiary classes. This enactment predates FATF's February 2023 R25 revision. [S12][S12].

**Facts:** Revised R24 implementation guidance calls for company-held information plus a registry or an alternative mechanism giving authorities rapid reliable access. CDD records alone do not constitute that alternative mechanism, and access to ownership information does not remove institutions' own CDD. It does not mandate a publicly searchable central registry. [2023 guidance][S11]. Revised R25 is wider than customer identification: it concerns resident or locally administering trustees/equivalents holding arrangement/party ownership information. Its 2024 guidance includes professional and non-professional trustees. [Recommendations][S15], [guidance][S16].

**Facts:** Hong Kong's SCR is held by a company and is not delivered to CR for registration. The current FAQ distinguishes covered local/re-domiciled companies from ordinary registered non-Hong Kong companies, includes control tests and nominee attribution, and provides designated law-enforcement access. [S13][S13]. The inward re-domiciliation regime took effect on 23 May 2025. [S14][S14].

**Analysis:** Registry data, beneficial-owner definitions and the obligations of trustees are different parts of the framework. No source reviewed here establishes comprehensive local transposition dates for revised R24/R25. Conversely, absence of a located instrument is not proof that no other relevant measure exists.

## Market Trends

**Facts:** The displayed 2023 technical table has partial ratings for R12, R15, R22 and R25; R16/R24 are largely compliant. Effectiveness is labelled 2019, with six substantial and five moderate outcomes. [S01][S01]. The 2023 follow-up upgraded R28 to largely compliant and retained regular follow-up. [S02][S02].

**Analysis:** Dated country ratings are neither a certificate for a current firm's controls nor a software-quality score. Preparation for a future assessment may create work, but the calendar does not establish procurement budgets. Institutions need evidence of effective review, reporting and remediation as well as correct written rules.

**Fact:** The fifth-round methodology groups IO3 around financial institutions/VASPs and IO4 around DNFBPs. [S04][S04]. Historical 2019 outcome labels should not be interpreted using these new groupings.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** Existing compliance teams and training providers may value a bounded rule-change review service connecting a source change to specific operational controls and dated teaching cases. This should reuse authoritative sources and current workflow tools. A generic stream of legal headlines is unlikely to prove enough value without an identified review bottleneck.

**Recommended next steps:** Interview compliance owners about version mistakes, update ownership and control testing. Compare with existing regulatory-intelligence offers. Test whether reviewers can distinguish current local law, adopted future standards, draft guidance and historical cases. Measure wrong-rule decisions, traceability, update time and reproducibility; validate source/data access and commercial demand before implementation.

**TwinAML proposals:** Provide a historical remittance alongside a later standard revision and require the learner to choose the relevant version; distinguish a trust-customer CDD task from a trustee-transparency duty; ask what a company-held SCR can and cannot establish. Keep statutory scope/effective dates separate from synthetic classroom thresholds. No new rule engine or scenario was implemented.
