# Hong Kong Payments and Digital-Bank Controls

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update adds payment-specific onboarding/outsourcing evidence and named operating monitoring systems to the [baseline technology map](2026-10-13-baseline.md#aml-technology-landscape). Fraud prevention, AML investigation and suspicious-report quality are related but require different evidence of effectiveness.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** HKMA's SVF register, revised 14 August 2026, lists 11 non-bank licensees and four banks issuing/facilitating SVFs. A payment product's licence depends on its entity and activities. [Register][S01], [consumer explanation][S02].
- **Fact:** HKMA's July 2025 33 Financial Services action concerned earlier onboarding/partner-control failures, including large gift-card purchases and individuals acting for businesses. [Statement][S07].
- **Fact:** ZA Bank describes its proprietary XDecision monitoring system in operation; throughput and speed are bank-reported, not independently validated AML accuracy. [Bank disclosure][S10].
- **Fact:** AlipayHK describes existing AI fraud monitoring. A separate announced EasySafePay adoption programme and trial claim do not establish a completed local deployment or AML benefit. [Issuer release][S12].
- **Analysis:** Concrete opportunities concern party identification, outsourced evidence, payment exceptions and reasoned escalation. The sources establish neither an unserved market nor buyer budgets.

### 33 Financial Services: a specific onboarding case

**Facts:** On 22 July 2025 HKMA reprimanded 33FS and imposed HK$1.6 million under the PSSVFO. The relevant period was 1 December 2019–31 August 2023. One corporate and five individual customers bought over 49,000 gift cards, about HK$25 million. Purpose information was blank/vague, actual usage differed from the recorded explanation, and some customers used different identities. Another ten individuals acted for businesses but were classified as beneficial owners because forms bore their names; CDD on the represented businesses was omitted. Controls did not ensure/document partner-performed face-to-face identity checks for non-Hong Kong residents under internal requirements when no remote solution was used. Remediation and cooperation were acknowledged. [Complete disciplinary statement][S07].

**Analysis:** A named person on an application is not necessarily the actual business customer or beneficial owner. Training can test the purpose, representation and retained partner evidence before any transaction-pattern score is interpreted. The findings do not identify a software vendor/defect, prove laundering or establish current unremediated failures.

### MSO evidence completeness

**Fact:** Gold Town Investment Limited's decision dated 27 November 2025, published 19 December, imposed a reprimand/remedial action for missing remittance addresses/timestamps, ineffective PEP procedures, missing records and unnotified changes involving ten bank accounts. No monetary penalty is stated for Gold Town; other pages in the PDF concern other firms. [Statement page 1][S08]. The [June 2026 decisions in update 6](2026-10-13-update-06.md#latest-regulatory-developments) provide further named timestamp/PEP examples.

## Market Trends

**Facts:** HKMA's 2025 report records 86.4 million SVF accounts, 8.6 billion transactions and HK$1,115.5 billion transaction value. [Printed page 145][S04]. Accounts are not unique people or software buyers, and transaction value does not disclose AML spending.

| Buyer/activity class | Verified distinction | Research implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-bank SVF | Multipurpose stored value for third-party merchants/P2P falls within the SVF regime; payment methods without stored value fall outside that regime. [S02][S02]. | Map the actual product and issuer before applying SVF requirements. |
| MSO | Money changing/remittance is a separate AMLO regime. [Official preventive-measures map][S03]. | Review remittance fields, records and channels against this scope. |
| Digital bank | Bank supervision applies, adapted appropriately to the model; an app label does not establish a licence. [HKMA paragraph 11][S16]. | Review account controls and investigations as banking operations. |
| Gateway/acquirer/intermediary | These are business roles. RPS guidance distinguishes operators, participants and service agents with due diligence/contracts in their relationships. [S15][S15]. | Identify entity, licences and participant relationships before assigning duties. |

**Analysis:** Treating every payment firm as an SVF would misclassify buyers and controls. Corporate groups can also contain separate MSO/SVF entities; brand-level capability or licensing statements should not be transferred across entities without evidence.

| Named relationship / source date | Existing solution and strength evidenced | Weakness or status boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ZA Bank XDecision, 30 September 2025 | Bank describes proprietary real-time monitoring: over 600,000 daily risk assessments, 90% within 0.2 seconds. Disclosure generally uses 30 June 2025 data. [S10][S10]. | Self-reported throughput/latency; no independent AML precision/recall, STR-quality or savings evaluation. |
| ZA Bank explanation, 22 December 2025 | Describes continuing transaction review/stooge-account analysis, over 1.3 million daily reviews and potential pauses/restrictions/investigations. [S11][S11]. | The labels/snapshots differ from the September article. Do not infer a growth rate or measured fraud prevalence from its illustrations. |
| AlipayHK / Ant International, 11 September 2025 | AlipayHK CEO describes existing 24/7 AI monitoring, configurable protections and education. [S12][S12]. | EasySafePay 360 adoption is scheduled in this announcement; its unspecified 90% account-takeover trial result is not a verified AlipayHK AML result. |
| Mox, 20 March 2025 | Bank describes AI account monitoring and Police recognition concerning STR quality/upstream scam intervention. [S13][S13]. | No named engine/vendor, evaluation protocol, quantified reduction or spend is disclosed. Recognition is narrower evidence than technical validation. |
| FCC Analytics / WeLab, dated 1 November 2021 | Vendor records a transaction-monitoring PoC under the subsidy scheme. [S14][S14]. | A homepage use-case label does not turn this into a 2025–2026 production announcement; its saving claim lacks a disclosed method. |

**Analysis:** These examples add operating proprietary systems and mature incumbent fraud controls to the earlier partnership/pilot evidence. An AML product comparison needs tasks and outcomes that matter: quality of resolved review, escalation reasons, investigation completeness and maintained customer protection. Processing faster or reducing account takeover is not by itself measured laundering detection.

**Outsourcing implication:** A control owner needs evidence that a provider actually performed the promised checks, accessible records and a documented resolution of exceptions. The SVF Practice Note does not establish a blanket prohibition on overseas hosting. [S06][S06]. A proposed cloud assistant still requires assessment of its actual data and responsibilities, as discussed in [update 4](2026-10-13-update-04.md).

## Business Opportunities

**Hypotheses and target customers:** For SVFs, test an onboarding/partner-evidence training service focused on business representation, intended use and exceptions. For MSOs, test retained remittance metadata/change-review exercises. For banks, test reasoning from a risk alert to proportionate information gathering and escalation. These are different workflows; regulatory similarity alone does not prove one product will fit all three.

**Recommended next steps:** Interview operations and compliance owners about review volumes, repeated evidence gaps, current tools and budgets. Compare with existing workflow and monitoring systems. Use synthetic test cases before seeking real data. Measure complete files, missed representation/authority issues, unjustified restrictions, escalation reasoning and time to resolution; separately measure any fraud outcome. Establish a baseline and independent review before repeating a vendor's ROI claim.

**TwinAML proposal:** An individual requests a large gift-card purchase “for trading,” acting for a company. Release evidence gradually: application purpose, usage discrepancy, represented entity and partner identity-check record. The learner identifies the right parties and decides what remains unresolved, with complete/legitimate comparison cases to test over-escalation. Keep the classroom rule and historical case facts distinguishable. This update implements no new case or application.
