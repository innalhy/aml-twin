# Hong Kong AML Competence and Training Evidence

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update develops the training opportunity in [update 4](2026-10-13-update-04.md) with current qualification, assessment and transition evidence. It does not establish accreditation, buyer demand or a learning benefit for TwinAML.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** HKMA describes the ECF as a competency framework, not a mandatory licensing regime. [Overview][S01].
- **Fact:** The current linked AML/CFT guide is the March 2018 edition, which replaces the December 2016 version. [Guide][S02], [current record][S06].
- **Fact:** HKIB's Professional assessment already includes essay/case-study questions and its syllabus includes scenarios/discussion. [Handbook][S03].
- **Analysis:** The commercial question is whether a supplementary practice method improves a specific task beyond existing training. An unoccupied market cannot be inferred from a simulator idea.

The separate proposed-structure annex was not used here. No requirement to purchase a particular learning product follows from the reviewed material.

## Market Trends

**Facts:** HKIB's handbook, updated 28 May 2026, lists the following existing programme structure. Training fees are not total employer software budgets, and lecture hours are not total learning time. [S03][S03].

| Published item | Core | Professional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lecture hours | 15 | 18 |
| Training fee | HK$4,400 | HK$5,000 |
| Recommended total learning hours | At least 200 | At least 300 |
| Exam duration | 2.5 hours | 3 hours |
| Questions | 80 MCQs | 50 MCQs plus two of three essay/case-study questions |
| Pass mark | 70% | 60% |

**Analysis:** Fees provide a dated comparison with an existing purchase category. They neither reveal the price buyers would pay for extra practice nor establish the simulator's addressable market. An exercise also competes for learner and instructor time, so evaluation needs to include case preparation and review effort.

No controlled learning comparison, private employer price or product accreditation was verified. The course structure must not be reduced to “all existing training is multiple choice”.

## AML Technology Landscape

**Fact:** The 2018 guide allows verified e-learning within its CPD arrangements and addresses retained training/exam/CPD records. It does not authorise an independent product to award its professional designations. [S02][S02].

| Proposed practice task | Observable learner work | Appropriate limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compare activity with a customer profile | Supported links between facts and the explanation. | Unusual activity alone does not prove laundering. |
| Request further information | Relevant questions, missed evidence and unnecessary requests. | More requests are not automatically better diligence. |
| Resolve conflicting records | Sources checked, dates and remaining uncertainty. | A fluent answer cannot substitute for evidence. |
| Write an escalation or closure | Accurate facts, policy-grounded reasoning and sufficient context. | Report quantity alone cannot show quality. |
| Revise a decision | Justified correction after fresh evidence. | Rewarding an unchanged first guess can encourage hindsight errors. |

These are evaluation proposals. They do not imply that HKMA requires a configurable simulator or that TwinAML currently provides every task in the table.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** Potential collaborators include established course providers, bank learning teams and AML managers who can identify a specific competency needing more practice. Supplementary case work may be useful if instructors can author, review and assess it efficiently. Those buying and delivery assumptions require validation.

**Recommended pilot:** Use paired suspicious/legitimate cases and an expert rubric. Compare equivalent training time; blind the scoring where feasible; report sample size, attrition, reviewer disagreement and the rubric. Assess fresh cases and later retention rather than repeated memorised questions. Track time alongside reasoning quality and mistaken escalation/closure.

**Recommended commercial next step:** Establish who owns the budget, what existing materials fail to practise, what acceptance evidence is required, and whether records can fit the buyer's current process. A course fee, completion count or promising demo does not settle these questions.

**TwinAML scope:** Reuse its current evidence choices and retained decisions for a small synthetic evaluation if later authorised. Do not add a second policy/state system or claim training credits without the relevant provider's acceptance. No curriculum, product feature, live learner study or accreditation application was created in this research pass.
