# Hong Kong Financial-Intelligence Technology: Procurement and Delivery

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update adds official contract awards to the [baseline software map](2026-10-13-baseline.md#aml-technology-landscape). It corrects the statement that no STREAMS 2 contractor was identified and adds a newer, specific FDAP delivery milestone. A contract award and an operational system are separate evidence.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** DPO's official award dataset identifies NCSI (HK) Limited for STREAMS 2 development and a later interface/enhancement assignment. Other named Police assignments cover data management, FINEST enhancement and analytics support. [Dataset][S01].
- **Fact:** A February 2026 LegCo paper states that TAS launched in January 2025, while other FDAP tools/systems remained in phased development. [LegCo][S04].
- **Fact:** The relevant SOA-QPS procurement route uses a contractor framework and defined assignment limits. It is not an unrestricted invitation for a startup to supply JFIU. [DPO][S02].
- **Analysis:** These records establish specific public purchases. They do not establish the market size for AML software or demand for a training simulator.

**Naming limit:** The award calls its supported system “Transaction Analytics System”; the operational paper uses “Transactions Analytic System”. Correspondence is plausible, but the reviewed sources do not explicitly map that contract to the operational milestone. Retain the two source labels when precision matters.

**Analysis:** A learner-facing investigation view could model reported evidence, intelligence triage and fund-flow reasoning without claiming access to any Police system. Public infrastructure is prior art and context, not an integration available to TwinAML.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** A synthetic training tool could teach how to distinguish a transaction record, a linked entity, an unresolved explanation, an intelligence lead and admissible evidence. Potential users include compliance training teams and educators. These purchases do not establish their willingness to buy that tool.

**Recommended next steps:** Compare the proposed learning tasks with existing regulator/vendor training, interview instructors about assessment gaps, and evaluate a small synthetic investigation exercise. Separately assess procurement eligibility before proposing public-sector sales. No government-system connection, tender, supplier contact or application was made.

**Further delivery checks:** Retrieve acceptance or programme progress evidence for enhancements, not just awards. Match each title and legal entity rather than assuming one contractor supplied the entire programme. Current detailed specifications, subcontracting and delivered software versions remain unverified.
