## Research method

- Use regulator, government, court, and legislative publications for requirements, decisions, and dates. Check legal commencement separately from announcement, consultation, and planned implementation.
- Use bank/customer announcements where available for deployment evidence. Attribute vendor claims, preserve the customer/entity and function, and distinguish a partnership, pilot, production deployment, and reported performance.
- Separate facts, analysis, and recommendations. A plausible product idea is not established demand, market size, or regulatory approval.
- Distinguish monitoring alerts, suspicious reports, account controls, investigation, arrest, charge, conviction, restraint, and confiscation.
- Date every update and compare it with earlier findings. Preserve substantive historical findings; correct mistakes explicitly and retain unresolved verification leads.
- Keep work within research notes unless the user separately asks for implementation. TwinAML's synthetic thresholds remain classroom policy, and its research must not imply a live bank connection or official STR submission.

## Topics for continuing research

Track HKMA, SFC, IA, Companies Registry, FSTB, and JFIU, with Customs, EAA, Police, DOJ, LegCo, and FATF where relevant. Cover banks and virtual banks; FinTech and payments; VA firms; insurance; securities and asset management; family offices and wealth; real estate; and high-value goods.

Prioritise material changes to obligations, guidance, enforcement, official statistics, and verified deployments. Keep new product hypotheses tied to a documented problem and identify the customer research needed to test them.
