# Hong Kong Recruitment Conduct and Forged Payment Evidence

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update adds two official case summaries to the evidence-integrity discussion in [update 14](2026-10-13-update-14.md). It preserves each reported offence and outcome rather than classifying every financial-crime case as a completed laundering transaction.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** The DoJ published its 2025 prosecution review on 19 May 2026. [Release][S01].
- **Fact:** Its case summaries distinguish account-recruitment incitement from use of forged payment instruments. [Review][S02].
- **Analysis:** Investigation training can address preparatory conduct and document authenticity while leaving criminal conclusions to their proper evidential process.

## Market Trends

**Analysis:** The cases provide concrete patterns for investigation exercises; they do not establish prevalence or a trend rate. They also demonstrate why an outcome field should preserve the actual offence and procedural stage. Counting a recruitment case as money already laundered would distort both the case description and any derived “detection” total.

The separate statistical denominators in [update 18](2026-10-13-update-18.md) remain applicable: neither a short case summary nor annual aggregate convictions supplies a product-performance metric.

## AML Technology Landscape

| Proposed review capability | Useful task | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chronology with source links | Establish what the available evidence supports at each point. | Later case outcomes must not be silently imported into an earlier review. |
| Communication evidence review | Test the connection between a message, the parties and the conduct alleged. | A search term or social-media association alone cannot prove participation. |
| Payment-document verification | Distinguish a document's appearance from supported authenticity and settlement evidence. | OCR or a plausible generated explanation cannot authenticate a bank instrument. |
| Outcome labels | Preserve allegation, investigation, charge, offence, sentence and appeal separately. | One generic “money laundering detected” status loses material distinctions. |

These are proposed analytical capabilities. The reviewed summaries do not identify a named commercial detection system or show that its output initiated either case.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** Educators and investigation reviewers may value paired exercises where suspicious-looking material has different supported explanations and different procedural outcomes. A learner should be assessed on enquiries, provenance, uncertainty and the applicable reporting route, not on predicting a conviction from the first clue.

**Recommended next steps:** Review existing training cases for hindsight leakage and unsupported offence labels. Compare a synthetic document/contact exercise with current materials, then test whether learners identify the missing evidence before drawing a conclusion. Buyer demand and learning benefit remain unmeasured.

**TwinAML scope:** A proposed exercise can reuse existing evidence cards and timeline events. It should label synthetic legal outcomes as authored scenario outcomes and retain the facts available at the decision time. This research update creates no new app feature or real case dataset.
