# Hong Kong Bank Information Sharing: Corrections and Independent Decisions

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update expands the [baseline bank-sharing account](2026-10-13-baseline.md#latest-regulatory-developments) and adds January 2026 joint HKMA/PCPD supervision. Here “AI” in “AI-to-AI” means authorised institution, not artificial intelligence.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** The November 2025 guideline limits the bank-sharing gateway and preserves PDPO relevance. It is not a general licence to exchange customer data. [HKMA][S01].
- **Fact:** Inaccurate shared information requires correction; recipients must make their own assessment rather than treat a shared name as an automatic blacklist. [HKMA][S01].
- **Fact:** Posting through the gateway does not replace an STR. [HKMA][S01].
- **Fact:** HKMA and PCPD announced joint selected-bank examinations of anti-fraud controls and data safeguards in January 2026. [Joint statement][S03].
- **Analysis:** A valuable exercise should include an erroneous association, its correction and the consequences for prior decisions, rather than assuming shared intelligence is infallible.

## Latest Regulatory Developments

| Control / development | Impact | Who is affected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November 2025 information-sharing guideline | Approved institutions/channels, purpose and request/disclosure conditions matter. Safe-harbour protection is conditional on good faith and reasonable care; PDPO still applies. [S01][S01]. | Participating banks and their authorised reviewers. |
| Correction and use requirements, Chapters 9–10 | Correct inaccuracies promptly and pass corrections to recipients; assess shared information alongside other evidence. [S01][S01]. | Disclosing/receiving banks and teams maintaining downstream records. |
| Joint HKMA/PCPD announcement, 27 January 2026 | Selected-bank risk-based examinations cover implementation of earlier anti-fraud measures alongside data security/access controls. [S03][S03]. | Banks selected for examination; industry reviewing the relevant safeguards. |

**Facts:** Chapter 13 distinguishes gateway posting from statutory STR filing. Chapter 9 covers updating monitoring/screening records after corrections, including onward recipients. Chapter 10 rejects automatic exclusions based solely on a shared name. [S01][S01].

**Analysis:** A sharing event, suspicion report, account restriction and record correction are separate actions with separate reasons. A simulator should not collapse them into one “reported” flag. The [baseline's no-consent discussion](2026-10-13-baseline.md#latest-regulatory-developments) is a further distinct legal/control question.

## Market Trends

**Facts:** The January joint statement links more sophisticated fraud with personal-data misuse. HKMA's examinations focus on anti-fraud implementation; PCPD reviews safeguards against misuse/leakage. The programme also includes joint education and work with Police, HKAB and industry. It reports planned measures, not examination outcomes or penalties. [S03][S03].

**Analysis:** More sharing can improve context while also spreading errors faster. Useful assurance therefore includes timeliness, relevance, access, correction propagation and the quality of resulting decisions. Counting shared records alone would miss both successful review and harmful mistaken association.

**Change from earlier research:** The baseline established the gateway and FINEST enhancement. This update adds correction/use controls and current joint-supervision evidence. [Update 13](2026-10-13-update-13.md) separately provides procurement records; neither source establishes an unrestricted third-party integration for TwinAML.

## AML Technology Landscape

| Existing mechanism | Potential strength | Failure or evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secure bank-sharing platform | Supplies context beyond one institution's records. | Shared information can be incomplete, inaccurate or superseded. |
| Original and corrected record history | Can explain what was known when a decision was taken and what subsequently changed. | Retaining history must be reconciled with corrections and confidentiality; an obsolete label should not silently continue driving live review. |
| Monitoring/screening reconciliation | Can connect a corrected identity/association with affected alerts and cases. | A correction notice is insufficient if downstream reviewers continue using stale conclusions. |
| Reviewer assessment | Can reconcile intelligence with account records and explanations. | A shared identifier alone cannot settle every control decision. |

The table is an analytical comparison. It does not establish a new mandatory software architecture, named supplier capability or measured market gap.

**Data quality question:** Distinguish the underlying observation from the interpretation. A device identifier, account link or name candidate may be accurately observed while the conclusion about who controlled it is uncertain. A reviewable case should preserve that distinction and show which later fact changed the assessment.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** Bank compliance training could evaluate whether reviewers recognise uncertain associations, seek corroboration, issue an appropriate correction and reassess affected decisions. Potential buyers include AML, fraud, data-governance and quality-assurance teams; the relative value to each is unmeasured.

**Recommended next steps:** Interview teams about inaccurate intelligence and correction hand-offs. Compare current platform procedures and internal case-management tools before proposing a separate product. Test a synthetic exercise in which the first disclosure appears credible, later identifiers distinguish two people and a receiving institution has already acted. Measure propagation completeness, reassessment quality, avoidable restrictions and handling time.

**TwinAML proposal:** Model disclosure, receipt, enquiry, correction and review as dated events, with each learner decision tied to the evidence then available. Keep reporting and account-control choices separate. No real data was shared, no platform access was obtained and no customer decision was made.
