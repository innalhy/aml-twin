# Hong Kong STR Quality and Outcome Denominators

Research date: 13 October 2026. This update adds JFIU reporting-quality detail and qualifies aggregate outcomes in the [baseline](2026-10-13-baseline.md#market-trends). It does not establish a matched report-to-conviction cohort or a software detection metric.

## Executive Summary

- **Fact:** JFIU's educational material identifies unsupported subject grouping, attachment-only narratives and copying another group company's reporting decision as pitfalls. [Webinar][S02].
- **Fact:** The current JFIU outcome table reports 925 persons convicted in 2025; Police Review reports 923. The reviewed sources do not reconcile them. [JFIU][S04], [Police][S05].
- **Fact:** Report dissemination, system searches, persons convicted and property values have different units and scopes. [Annual report][S01], [outcome table][S04].
- **Analysis:** Training can evaluate investigation/report quality without inventing a prosecution result. Annual totals do not supply classifier precision or causal attribution.

## Market Trends

| Published measure | Result | Unit / evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2024 reports received / disseminated | 147,660 / 45,376; reported dissemination rate 30.7%. [S01][S01]. | Reports, not persons convicted or unique investigations. |
| 2024 direct searches / record checks | 334,661 / 2,508. [S01][S01]. | Search/check events, not unique successful detections. |
| 2025 ML convictions in JFIU table | 925 persons. [S04][S04]. | HKPF/C&ED figures under the table's stated ordinances and cut-off. |
| 2025 assets restrained | HK$261,299,421. [S04][S04]. | DoJ property-value series. |
| 2025 recovered/paid to government | HK$125,286,726. [S04][S04]. | DoJ recovery-value series; not a matched restrained cohort. |

**Unresolved source difference:** Police Review separately reports 923 and labels its approximate HK$125 million amount “confiscated”; JFIU uses “recovered & paid to Gov't”. Preserve each source's label. Do not infer that the two-person difference represents Customs cases or that the monetary categories are identical. [S05][S05], [S04][S04].

**Analysis:** Dividing conviction persons by STR reports would not measure detection yield. Dividing annual recovered value by restrained value would not establish a recovery rate. Timing, population, units and case linkage are missing. A report can inform multiple enquiries, and an enquiry can use multiple sources; attribution remains unestablished without linked evidence.

## AML Technology Landscape

**Facts:** JFIU describes prioritisation by inherent risk, intelligence value and actionable potential. Quantitative/qualitative feedback and quarterly analysis are available in the reporting platform's Secure Area. The restricted reports were not accessed here. [S01][S01].

**Facts:** The webinar asks for review period, party links, KYC/enquiry results, source links and available digital footprints. It uses pass-through brokerage activity without trading and shared account-contact/IP information as educational indicators. These are typologies, not performance benchmarks. [S02][S02].

| Review output | Potential value | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account/event chronology | Makes the period and sequence intelligible. | A neat timeline does not establish a criminal predicate. |
| Entity-link explanation | Distinguishes supported connections from coincidental similarity. | An unexplained grouping can contaminate the narrative. |
| Report narrative with supporting records | Makes reasons and evidence accessible to the next reviewer. | Attachments alone cannot explain the reporting decision. |
| Metric glossary | Makes scope and units visible. | Public aggregates remain unsuitable for product accuracy or attribution claims. |

The comparison is analysis, not a requirement to buy a particular report-writing product.

## Business Opportunities

**Hypothesis:** A synthetic exercise could assess identification, enquiry, explanation testing, escalation and narrative quality separately. Potential users are compliance educators and reporting/QA teams. Demand and measurable learning impact remain unverified.

**Recommended next steps:** Compare current training and report-review practices; interview MLROs about omissions and unsupported links. Evaluate synthetic reports on narrative completeness, evidence relevance, correct uncertainty and appropriate escalation. Label exercise outcomes as exercise performance, separately from real detection effectiveness.

**TwinAML proposal:** A case timeline can include acknowledgement, additional-information requests and later investigative evidence with branching outcomes and explicit unknowns. It should not award a fictitious conviction for every filed report or claim that public annual recoveries were produced by the simulator. No real STR, restricted feedback or live reporting connection was accessed.
