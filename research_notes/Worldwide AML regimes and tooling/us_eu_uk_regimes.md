# AML regimes of the United States, European Union and United Kingdom (status as of October 2026)

Status labels used throughout: **[IN FORCE]**, **[PROPOSED]**, **[DELAYED]**, **[VACATED]**, **[TRANSITION]**, **[ENACTED, NOT YET APPLICABLE]**. Research date: 13 October 2026. Many late-2026 status points come from law-firm or vendor summaries because primary pages could not be fetched. Those points are flagged.

## United States: BSA framework, AMLA 2020 rules, deregulatory shift

### Takeaway
The US regime is still the rules-based Bank Secrecy Act (BSA): $10,000 CTRs, SARs with dollar thresholds, and no consent mechanism. FinCEN writes the rules, the banking agencies examine banks, and DOJ/FinCEN/OCC/Fed enforce. In 2025-2026 the AMLA 2020 agenda turned deregulatory. CTA beneficial ownership reporting for US companies is permanently ended. The investment adviser rule is delayed to 2028. The residential real estate rule was vacated in court. A new April 2026 program-rule proposal replaces the 2024 draft. Raising the SAR/CTR thresholds is being discussed but has not been enacted.

### Cited Findings
**BSA basics and thresholds**
- **[IN FORCE]** Banks file a CTR for cash transactions over $10,000. Bank SAR thresholds are $5,000 for known suspects and $25,000 otherwise. The SAR is due 30 days after initial detection, and records are kept 5 years. — [31 CFR 1010.311 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-C/section-1010.311); [31 CFR 1020.320 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1020/subpart-C/section-1020.320)
- Thresholds remain $10,000 (CTR) and $5,000 (bank SAR). Advocates note that the $5,000 SAR threshold has not been updated since 1992. — [America's Credit Unions](https://www.americascreditunions.org/news-media/news/updated-ctrsar-thresholds-would-be-targeted-commonsense-regulatory-reform)
- **[IN FORCE, guidance]** On 9 October 2025, FinCEN and the federal banking agencies issued SAR FAQs. They say that transactions at or near $10,000 are not, on their own, enough to require a SAR. They also say there is no requirement to file continuing-activity SARs after a 90-day period. — [FinCEN news release](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-issues-frequently-asked-questions-clarify-suspicious-activity-reporting); [FinCEN SAR FAQs Oct 2025 (PDF)](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2025-10/SAR-FAQs-October-2025.pdf); [WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20251028-fincen-clarifies-suspicious-activity-reporting-requirements)

**SAR/CTR threshold reform**
- **[PROPOSED, bill only]** The STREAMLINE Act was introduced in October 2025 by Sens. Tim Scott and John Kennedy. It would raise the CTR threshold from $10,000 to $30,000, raise the SAR thresholds from $2,000 to $3,000 and from $5,000 to $10,000, and index the CTR threshold to inflation every 5 years. — [ACAMS](https://www.acams.org/en/news/us-senate-bill-would-revise-sar-ctr-thresholds); [ICBA](https://www.icba.org/w/senate-panel-touts-support-for-bill-to-raise-ctr-sar-thresholds)
- **[PROPOSED, bill only]** H.R. 1799, the Financial Reporting Threshold Modernization Act, would also raise the thresholds, following a GAO recommendation. — [ICBA](https://www.icba.org/w/senate-panel-touts-support-for-bill-to-raise-ctr-sar-thresholds)
- On 15 September 2026, Treasury Secretary Bessent reportedly told House Financial Services he would consider higher thresholds. This comes from an advocacy letter and is unverified. — [DCUC letter, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.dcuc.org/uploads/1/4/5/1/145178789/dcuc_letter_to_sec._bessent_ctr_reform_9.16.26.pdf)

**AML/CFT program rule (AMLA 2020 s.6101)**
- **[PROPOSED]** FinCEN issued a new program NPRM on 7 April 2026, published 10 April 2026 at 91 Fed. Reg. 18,704. It supersedes the July 2024 proposal. Comments closed 9 June 2026. No final rule has been issued. If finalized, institutions would have about 12 months to comply. — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/10/2026-07033/anti-money-laundering-and-countering-the-financing-of-terrorism-programs); [FinCEN NPRM PDF](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-04/Program-NPRM.pdf); [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/out-with-the-old-in-with-the-risk-based-fincen-proposes-fundamental-reform-of-aml-cft-program-requirements)
- The NPRM uses a two-prong framework that separates program "establishment" from "maintenance". Banking regulators would have to give FinCEN at least 30 days' notice before a "significant supervisory action". It emphasizes effectiveness over technical compliance and ties risk assessment to the National AML/CFT Priorities. — [Gibson Dunn mid-year 2026](https://www.gibsondunn.com/mid-year-developments-in-anti-money-laundering-in-2026/); [Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/insights/memo/2026/April/Regulators-Issue-Proposed-Rules-Reforming-AML-CFT-Program-Requirements); [Miller & Chevalier](https://www.millerchevalier.com/publication/fincen-proposes-updates-amlcft-program-requirements)

**Corporate Transparency Act / beneficial ownership**
- **[IN FORCE]** The 26 March 2025 interim final rule (90 Fed. Reg. 13,688) narrowed BOI reporting to foreign companies registered to do business in the US. — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/mid-year-developments-in-anti-money-laundering-in-2026/)
- **[IN FORCE, final]** FinCEN issued a final rule on 11 August 2026, effective on Federal Register publication around 14 August 2026. It permanently exempts all domestic reporting companies and US persons. Foreign reporting companies report only non-US beneficial owners, within 30 days of registration. FinCEN will delete BOI that US persons previously reported. Sources disagree on whether the rule merely adopts the IFR or expands it. — [Sidley](https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2026/08/us-fincen-issues-final-rule-ending-beneficial-ownership-reporting-requirement); [Vedder Price](https://www.vedder.com/insights-events/fincen-issues-final-rule-permanently-ending-beneficial-ownership-reporting-for-u-s-companies-and-u-s-persons/); [Dorsey](https://www.dorsey.com/newsresources/publications/client-alerts/2026/9/corporate-transparency-act)
- The bank Customer Due Diligence (CDD) beneficial ownership requirements remain separate and still apply. — [Winston Taylor](https://www.winstontaylor.com/insights/the-final-nail-in-the-corporate-transparency-act-coffin-fincen-finalizes-permanent-rollback-of-beneficial-ownership-reporting-for-domestic-companies-and-u-s-persons)

**Investment adviser AML rule**
- **[DELAYED]** A final rule published 2 January 2026 moved the effective date for RIAs and exempt reporting advisers from 1 January 2026 to **1 January 2028**. FinCEN plans to revisit the rule's scope in further rulemaking and coordinate it with the investment adviser CIP rule. — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/02/2025-24184/delaying-the-effective-date-of-the-anti-money-launderingcountering-the-financing-of-terrorism); [Proskauer](https://www.proskauer.com/blog/fincen-finalizes-twoyear-delay-of-the-investment-adviser-aml-rule-reaffirms-intent-to-further-review-and-tailor-the-rule-and-to-coordinate-with-other-rulemakings)

**Residential real estate reporting rule**
- **[VACATED]** The rule was published 29 August 2024. It was postponed from 1 December 2025 to 1 March 2026 and took effect on that date. On 19 March 2026, *Flowers Title Cos. v. Bessent* (E.D. Tex.) vacated it nationwide as exceeding FinCEN's BSA authority. FinCEN says filers face no current obligation or liability. Earlier rulings in M.D. Fla. (19 Feb 2026) and N.D. Tex. (25 Feb 2026) had upheld the rule. An appeal is pending in the 11th Circuit (Fidelity National Title, brief due 31 July 2026). Real estate Geographic Targeting Orders (GTOs) remain in effect. — [Gibson Dunn vacatur alert](https://www.gibsondunn.com/fincen-residential-real-estate-reporting-rule-vacated-nationwide/); [Gibson Dunn mid-year](https://www.gibsondunn.com/mid-year-developments-in-anti-money-laundering-in-2026/); [FinCEN postponement release](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-announces-postponement-residential-real-estate-reporting-until-march-1); [Foley](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/03/federal-court-vacates-fincen-residential-real-estate-reporting-rule/)

**Whistleblower program, information sharing, stablecoins**
- **[PROPOSED]** FinCEN's whistleblower NPRM was published 1 April 2026 at 91 Fed. Reg. 16,328. It covers the BSA, IEEPA, TWEA and the Kingpin Act. Awards of up to 30% would apply where sanctions exceed $1m. Compliance staff would wait 120 days before becoming eligible, and retaliation would be prohibited. — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/mid-year-developments-in-anti-money-laundering-in-2026/)
- **[IN FORCE, guidance]** FinCEN's 314(b) guidance of 12 June 2026 says fraud counts as a specified unlawful activity. Peer-to-peer sharing may rest on suspicion alone and may cover attempted transactions, and filers should note 314(b) reliance in SARs. — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/mid-year-developments-in-anti-money-laundering-in-2026/)
- **[PROPOSED]** The GENIUS Act stablecoin rules include a FinCEN/OFAC AML and sanctions program NPRM for payment stablecoin issuers (91 Fed. Reg. 18,582, 10 April 2026), a CIP NPRM (22 June 2026), and FDIC (5 June) and OCC (24 June) proposals. A final rule is expected to be effective by 18 January 2027. — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/mid-year-developments-in-anti-money-laundering-in-2026/); [BDO 2026 tracker](https://www.bdo.com/insights/advisory/2026-u-s-aml-cft-regulatory-developments-tracker)

**SAR volumes**
- FinCEN's FY2024 review says the top 10 filers accounted for about 45% of FY24 SARs. — [FinCEN FY2024 Year in Review (PDF)](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2025-08/FinCEN-Infographic-Public-2025-508.pdf)
- In calendar 2025, all filers submitted more than **4.105 million SARs**, up 7.99%. Depository institutions filed more than 2.193 million, up 7.66%. This is a secondary source citing FinCEN data. — [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.us/forsights/2026/03/suspicious-activity-report-sar-filings-hit-record-in-2025); FinCEN's official series currently runs through 2024: [FinCEN SAR Stats](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/reports/sar-stats/sar-filings-industry)

**Fines and enforcement**
- In October 2024, TD Bank pleaded guilty to BSA violations and money laundering conspiracy, with more than $3bn in total penalties. FinCEN's $1.3bn was a record for the agency. The DOJ portion is about $1.8bn per NBC or about $1.9bn per CFO Brew. The OCC added $450m and the Fed $123.5m. The bank failed to monitor $18.3 trillion of activity, and the monitorship is 3 or 4 years depending on the source. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/td-bank-pleads-guilty-money-laundering-case-will-pay-3-billion-penalti-rcna174912); [ICIJ](https://www.icij.org/investigations/fincen-files/td-bank-hit-with-3b-penalty-in-u-s-money-laundering-settlement/); [CFO Brew](https://www.cfobrew.com/stories/2024/10/11/usd3-billion-in-penalties-for-td-bank-for-money-laundering-violations)
- 2026 actions:
  - 6 March 2026: FinCEN fined Canaccord Genuity $80m, the largest BSA penalty ever against a broker-dealer.
  - 30 June 2026: DOJ reached a non-prosecution agreement with EagleBank for about $9.7m.
  - 24 April 2026: OCC consent order against Community Federal Savings Bank.

  — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/mid-year-developments-in-anti-money-laundering-in-2026/)
- In 2025, DOJ SDNY crypto cases included OKX (about $504m) and KuCoin (about $297m). These figures come from an aggregator and are unverified against DOJ releases. — [AML Watcher](https://amlwatcher.com/blog/biggest-aml-fines-of-2025/)

### Inferences
- The 2025-2026 US direction is deregulatory and focused on reducing filing burden: CTA rollback, the IA delay, SAR FAQs and threshold bills. Enforcement against willful failures still continues (Canaccord, EagleBank). The scale of the TD Bank case is the benchmark US AML fines are measured against.
- The US has no consent regime. Filing a SAR does not block a transaction or give legal cover to proceed. Institutions decide on their own whether to exit or continue.
- The April 2026 program NPRM would codify the AMLA 2020 standard of an "effective, risk-based, and reasonably designed" program. Until it is finalized, the existing pillar-based rules apply.

### Gaps
- FinCEN's official 2025 SAR total and the annual CTR volume were not retrieved. FinCEN has historically cited roughly 20 million CTRs a year, but I did not verify this in this session.
- I could not confirm the UBS Financial Services $125m FinCEN penalty (August 2026) against a primary source.
- I found no whistleblower award payout data and no final whistleblower rule.
- I did not confirm whether the 11th Circuit has ruled on the real estate appeal, or whether FinCEN appealed *Flowers Title* to the 5th Circuit.
- I did not research Fed, OCC or FDIC changes to "reputational risk" or BSA examinations in 2025-2026.

## European Union: 2024 AML package, AMLA, registers, crypto

### Takeaway
The EU is moving from directive-based minimum harmonisation to a directly applicable **single rulebook** (AMLR, Regulation 2024/1624, applying from **10 July 2027**). The package also includes AMLD6 (Directive 2024/1640) and **AMLA** in Frankfurt. AMLA has been operating since 2025 and will directly supervise up to 40 cross-border high-risk groups from **1 January 2028**, selected during 2027. The crypto travel rule (TFR 2023/1113) has applied since 30 December 2024.

### Cited Findings
- **[ENACTED, NOT YET APPLICABLE]**
  - The AMLR applies from 10 July 2027, except for football agents and professional clubs, which follow from 10 July 2029.
  - AMLD6 transposition is staged: Art. 74 by 10 July 2025; Arts. 11, 12, 13 and 15 (registers) by 10 July 2026; most provisions by 10 July 2027; Art. 18 (real-estate single access point) by 10 July 2029.
  - AMLD 2015/849 is repealed from 10 July 2027.

  — [Matheson AML timeline](https://publications.matheson.com/anti-money-laundering-timeline/); [Bird & Bird](https://twobirds.com/en/insights/2024/global/eu-aml-package-–-a-new-adventure-begins); [Freshfields navigator (PDF)](https://freshfields.com/4a4525/globalassets/noindex/documents/the-european-aml-package-a-navigator.pdf)
- "6AMLD" can refer to two instruments. Directive 2018/1673 is the criminal-law directive. AMLD6 (2024/1640) is the supervision and registers directive. Sources mix the names up. — [Crypto Law Profile / CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-laws/eu-aml-package-regulation-2024-1624-directive-2024-1640/)
- **Cash limit [ENACTED, applies July 2027]:** The AMLR sets an EU-wide limit of €10,000 on cash payments, and Member States may set lower limits. Sources disagree on whether the cap applies only to traders in goods and services or to all payments, so check Art. 80 AMLR. — [Didit AMLR explainer](https://didit.me/blog/amlr-explained-eu-aml-regulation-2027/); [Deloitte Legal](https://www.deloittelegal.de/dl/en/services/legal/perspectives/eu-aml-paket-wendepunkt-geldwaeschepraevention.html)
- **Beneficial ownership:**
  - The AMLR sets a harmonised threshold of 25% "or more", where the old test was "more than 25%". The Commission may lower it to 15% for high-risk sectors after an assessment due by 10 July 2029.
  - After the CJEU's November 2022 *WM and Sovim* judgment struck down general public access, access is being rebuilt on a "legitimate interest" basis. Journalists and civil society working on AML keep presumptive access.
  - Registers cannot serve as a standalone source for CDD verification.

  — [Zavia](https://zavia.ai/eu-beneficial-ownership-in-2026-amlr-amld6-amla-explained/); [Antaras](https://antaras-group.ch/beneficial-ownership-registers-in-europe-privacy-vs-compliance/) (secondary sources)
- **AMLA [IN FORCE, ramping up]:**
  - The Council appointed Chair Bruna Szego on 21 January 2025, and she started on 17 February 2025.
  - The fifth Executive Board member, Hennie Verbeek-Kusters, was confirmed by the EP in December 2025 and appointed by the Council on 23 February 2026, completing the board.
  - Planned staffing is about 450.

  — [AMLA governance](https://www.amla.europa.eu/governance_en); [eucrim](https://eucrim.eu/news/amla-executive-board-now-fully-operational-with-fifth-member-appointed/); [Brussels Morning](https://brusselsmorning.com/bruna-szego-appointed-chair-of-eu-money-laundering-authority/60847/)
- **Direct supervision selection:**
  - AMLA may select up to 40 credit or financial institutions or groups operating in at least 6 Member States, based on inherent risk, quality of controls and residual risk.
  - Selection takes place in 2027 and direct supervision starts 1 January 2028. AMLA's Single Programming Document targets full readiness for the first supervisory cycle by mid-2028.

  — [AMLA About](https://www.amla.europa.eu/about-amla_en); [AMLA SPD 2026-2028 (PDF)](https://www.amla.europa.eu/system/files/2026-02/AMLA%20SPD%202026-2028.pdf); [ACAMS](https://www.acams.org/en/news/amla-already-shortlisting-institutions-direct-supervision)
- **2026 preparatory steps:**
  - March-April 2026: AMLA tested and calibrated its risk models.
  - May 2026: it published a reporting package for national supervisors to identify provisionally eligible entities.
  - 15 August 2026: deadline for national supervisors' data collection.
  - End of September 2026: target to finalise the provisional eligibility list. I found no confirmation that the list was finalised.

  — [FIAU Malta](https://fiaumalta.org/news/amla-direct-supervision-key-preparatory-steps-ahead-of-2027-selection-exercise/); [iEU Monitoring](https://ieu-monitoring.com/editorial/amla-takes-next-step-toward-2027-selection-of-entities-for-direct-supervision/1215842)
- **EU high-risk third-country list [IN FORCE]:**
  - Delegated Regulation 2026/46 added Russia under a new category for FATF-suspended members, in force 29 January 2026.
  - Delegated Regulation 2026/83 added Bolivia and the British Virgin Islands and removed Burkina Faso, Mali, Mozambique, Nigeria, South Africa and Tanzania.
  - The earlier 2025/1184 update added Algeria, Angola, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya, Laos, Lebanon, Monaco and Namibia (from 5 August 2025) and removed Barbados and Gibraltar, among others.

  — [EUR-Lex 2026/83](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/83/oj/eng); [EUR-Lex 2026/46 (PDF)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:32026R0046); [European Commission](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/financial-crime/anti-money-laundering-and-countering-financing-terrorism-international-level_en)
- **Crypto [IN FORCE]:** TFR (EU) 2023/1113 applies from 30 December 2024. It extends the travel rule to crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) and requires risk assessment of transfers to and from self-hosted addresses. — [Matheson](https://publications.matheson.com/anti-money-laundering-timeline/); [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-laws/eu-aml-package-regulation-2024-1624-directive-2024-1640/)

### Inferences
- Until July 2027, EU supervision and reporting remain national: national FIUs receive STRs, national competent authorities supervise, and national laws transpose AMLD4 and AMLD5. The EU therefore has no single STR count or fine regime. AMLA is an overlay that coordinates FIUs and directly supervises only about 40 entities. It does not replace national supervisors.
- The AMLR moves the EU toward the US model of a detailed single rulebook. In style it stays closer to the UK risk-based approach, with obligations set by regulation, technical standards and guidelines.
- Some EU member states run postponement or freezing powers for FIUs, and AMLD6 harmonises FIU suspension powers. This is not a UK-style consent regime, but it is closer to one than the US model. Not verified this session; see Gaps.

### Gaps
- I found no EU-wide annual STR volume. Each national FIU publishes its own figures, and I did not retrieve an EU aggregate from Europol or the Commission's supranational risk assessment.
- I found no large 2025-2026 EU-level AML fines. BaFin results were securities-related, not AML. The largest European AML penalties, such as the 2024-2025 national cases, were not verified in this session.
- I did not confirm whether the AMLA provisional eligibility list was finalised by end-September 2026.
- I did not verify the exact Art. 80 AMLR cash-limit scope or the AMLD6 FIU suspension powers.

## United Kingdom: MLRs, POCA SARs and DAML, supervision reform, ECCTA

### Takeaway
The UK combines principles-based, risk-based MLRs 2017 (amended by **SI 2026/621**, mainly in force **30 June 2026**) with the POCA 2002 criminal framework. That framework includes a distinctive **consent regime (DAML)**: a reporter can obtain a statutory defence to proceed with a suspicious transaction. The UK is replacing 22 professional body supervisors with the **FCA as single professional services supervisor**, with a phased start in late 2028 and full transfer by 2030. ECCTA 2023 has added a failure-to-prevent-fraud offence (1 September 2025) and Companies House identity verification (18 November 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **[IN FORCE] Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (Amendment) Regulations 2026 (SI 2026/621):**
  - Passage: Lords approved 20 May 2026, Commons committee debated 3 June 2026, made 9 June 2026. Main provisions in force 30 June 2026. Some crypto provisions are deferred, such as enhanced due diligence (EDD) for third-country crypto correspondent relationships from 1 February 2027.
  - Changes:
    - EDD applies only to "unusually complex" transactions.
    - Mandatory country-based EDD is narrowed to FATF "call for action" countries.
    - The TCSP definition is extended to sales of off-the-shelf companies.

  — [legislation.gov.uk draft SI](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2026/9780348281743); [Explanatory memorandum (PDF)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2026/9780348281743/pdfs/ukdsiem_9780348281743_en_001.pdf); [UK Parliament SI timeline](https://statutoryinstruments.parliament.uk/instrument/8cBIMh8j/timeline/d9idLUBp); [STEP](https://www.step.org/industry-news/updated-uk-money-laundering-regulations-come-effect-30-june); [IPA](https://insolvency-practitioners.org.uk/the-money-laundering-terrorist-financing-amendment-regulations-2026-aml126/)
- **[IN FORCE] POCA consent regime:** A disclosure seeking "appropriate consent" (a DAML) gives the reporter a defence if:
  - the NCA does not refuse within a 7 working-day notice period, or
  - the NCA refuses, and the 31-day moratorium then expires (extendable by court).

  — [POCA 2002 s.335 (legislation.gov.uk)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29/section/335)
- **SAR volumes (UKFIU Annual Report 2024-25, April 2024 to March 2025):**
  - Total SARs: **866,616**, down 0.6% from 872,048. 97.86% were submitted via the portal.
  - DAML requests: **57,666**, up 1% from 57,081.
  - DAML refusals: a record **£382.6m** denied, up about 59%, including one case of £103m.
  - Average DAML turnaround fell from 3.11 days to 2.8 days.

  — [NCA UKFIU SARs Annual Report 2025 (PDF)](https://www.nationalcrimeagency.gov.uk/who-we-are/publications/786-sars-annual-report-2025/file); [IPA summary](https://insolvency-practitioners.org.uk/ukfiu-sars-annual-report-2024-25-and-updated-sars-guidance-newsletter126/); [Neopay](https://www.neopay.co.uk/ukfiu-annual-report-shows-denied-daml-requests-up-59-infographic/)
- **[TRANSITION] Supervision reform:**
  - HM Treasury decided the FCA will supervise legal, accountancy and TCSP firms, replacing 22 professional body supervisors. OPBAS (housed in the FCA) continues during the transition and then ceases in its current form.
  - The consultation on FCA powers closed 24 December 2025, and HMT responded in June 2026.
  - Primary legislation is going through the Financial Services and Markets Bill (Lords committee stage), with Royal Assent expected between September 2026 and March 2027. Further changes will come by amending the MLRs.
  - FCA timeline:
    - 2027: operating model review.
    - Late 2028: phased transition starts.
    - 2030: remaining PBS firms move.

  — [FCA AML supervisory reform page](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/aml-supervisory-reform); [HMT response (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68f609dc2f0fc56403a3d0c7/AML_Supervision_Reform_Response_Document_FINAL.pdf); [Travers Smith](https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/the-uk-s-amlctf-supervisory-reform-fca-to-become-sole-regulator-for-professional-services/); [AAT Sept 2026 update (PDF)](https://www.aat.org.uk/files/aml-supervisory-reform-update-for-members-september-2026.pdf); [Legal Futures](https://www.legalfutures.co.uk/latest-news/fca-to-start-taking-on-aml-oversight-from-2028)
- **[IN FORCE] ECCTA 2023, failure to prevent fraud:**
  - In force 1 September 2025.
  - Applies to large organisations meeting 2 of 3 tests: more than 250 employees, more than £36m turnover, more than £18m assets.
  - The defence is having reasonable prevention procedures. The penalty is an unlimited fine.

  — [Slaughter and May](https://www.slaughterandmay.com/insights/corporate-update/corporate-update-bulletin-11-september-2025/); [Safecall](https://www.safecall.co.uk/eccta/)
- **[IN FORCE, transition] Companies House identity verification:**
  - Voluntary from 8 April 2025.
  - Mandatory for new directors and PSCs from 18 November 2025.
  - Existing directors and PSCs have a 12-month transition, ending about November 2026.

  — [Ashfords](https://www.ashfords.co.uk/insights/articles/key-regulatory-and-compliance-updates-for-uk-businesses-this-autumn); [Taylor Wessing](https://www.taylorwessing.com/de/insights-and-events/insights/2025/04/new-directors-obligations-and-liabilities)
- **Fines:**

### Inferences
- **Structural contrasts across the three jurisdictions:**
  - **Consent and transaction blocking:**
    - UK: a SAR plus DAML gives a statutory defence, with NCA refusal and moratorium powers.
    - US: no consent mechanism. A SAR is a pure report with a safe harbour from liability for filing.
    - EU: FIU postponement powers vary nationally and are being harmonised under AMLD6.
  - **Thresholds:**
    - The US uses fixed dollar thresholds: CTR $10,000, SAR $5,000 or $2,000.
    - The UK SAR has no monetary threshold. Any knowledge or suspicion triggers it, and there is no CTR.
    - The EU has no CTR either, but the AMLR adds a €10,000 cash-payment ban.
  - **Rule style:**
    - The US is the most rules-based and prescriptive, though the 2026 NPRM pushes toward "effectiveness".
    - The UK MLRs are principles- and risk-based, with industry guidance (JMLSG) approved by HMT.
    - The EU is moving from minimum-harmonisation directives to a detailed directly applicable regulation.
  - **Supervisory architecture:**
    - US: FinCEN delegates exams to the OCC, Fed, FDIC, NCUA, SEC, FINRA and IRS.
    - UK: the FCA, HMRC, Gambling Commission and 22 professional body supervisors, consolidating into the FCA.
    - EU: national competent authorities, with AMLA directly supervising about 40 entities from 2028.
- **Volumes and fines.** The US files about 4.1m SARs a year (2025) against the UK's about 0.87m (2024-25), and US fines are an order of magnitude larger. TD Bank's $3bn+ compares with the largest recent FCA AML fines of about £20-45m.
- **Divergent beneficial ownership transparency.** The US rolled back CTA reporting for domestic companies. The UK is tightening Companies House identity verification, and the EU is keeping registers under legitimate-interest access.
