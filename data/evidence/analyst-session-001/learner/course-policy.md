# Fictional course investigation policy

This policy applies only to the TwinAML classroom exercise. Its alert thresholds and verification rules are authored assumptions, not a statement of law or a bank’s actual procedures.

## Identity verification

Quality observations such as lighting changes or irregular blink timing trigger review. They do not prove manipulation. An inconclusive capture is different from a failed challenge.

Two recorded failed challenges stop onboarding under this policy. Any exception must retain the original results and its reason, and must be reviewed. A successful repeat verification can resolve an inconclusive first capture when its log is independently checked.

## Payment alert

The NEW_ACCOUNT_CREDIT_AND_FANOUT alert is generated when an account opened within a day receives at least 300000 SCU and submits outgoing orders totalling at least 90 percent of that receipt within 30 minutes. This is an exercise alert rule; it is not a threshold for proving or reporting a crime.

Review the expected activity, payer, authority, recipients, documents, and payment status. Large amounts, rapid splits, a virtual-asset business category, or a potential name match each need context.

## Evidence and screening

Customer emails and submitted invoices are claims until checked. Use verified contact routes for independent confirmation. Resolve possible name matches using multiple identifiers. Resolving one match does not resolve unrelated concerns. These screening records use a wholly fictional watchlist.

## Provisional controls

An analyst may request an immediate provisional outbound hold during this exercise when a material identity or funds concern is unresolved. Record the concern, the time, and the next check. Apply the control proportionately and reconsider it when evidence arrives.

An effective hold prevents a pending order from executing at its due time. It does not undo a completed transfer. A drafted report or an escalation does not automatically place a hold.

When a temporary hold is released, an overdue order may execute immediately after an eligibility check. Check funds and order status again. At equal timestamps, an accepted hold is applied before an order’s due check. These timing conventions are classroom assumptions.

## Closure and escalation

Escalate a concern when material contradictions remain, explaining the evidence and uncertainty. Close a resolved alert with a reasoned note identifying the evidence that answers the concern. The exercise involves an internal draft and recommendation; it does not submit a report to any authority.

You are assessed on the information available when you decided. You are not expected to prove hidden scenario facts. A provisional review can be justified even when later checks resolve the alert.
